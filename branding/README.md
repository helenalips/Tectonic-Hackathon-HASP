# SD Worx Branding (for our HASP PoC)

Reference for making our prototype and pitch look and sound like SD Worx.

Source: [New look SD Worx – introducing the brand "makes work work"](https://www.sdworx.com/en-en/about-sd-worx/press/2026-06-25-new-look-sd-worx-introducing-brand-makes-work-work) (press release, 25 June 2026)

## 1. Brand idea

- **Tagline / brand idea:** *SD Worx makes work work.*
- **Positioning:** "Europe's backbone of work". It supports the essential, often unseen processes organisations rely on every day.
- **What it means:** SD Worx aligns employers, workers and regulations through intelligent solutions. It turns the complexity of work into confidence.
- **Pillars:** intelligent technology · deep expertise · reliable delivery · local knowledge
- **Why the refresh:** SD Worx has grown and now covers HR, Pay & Time. Customers face regulation, workforce shifts, new technology and rising expectations.
- **How it was built:** inside-out, with product, UX, marketing and regional teams.

Quotes from the release:
- "Our new design system is fluid, connected, and modern. Built to move with the world…" (Justine Kerr, Creative Agency Lead)
- "SD Worx has evolved by transforming the complexity of work into confidence…" (Ninia Daelman, Brand & Marketing Director)

## 2. Brand voice (derived from the release, not an official guide)

| Trait | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Confident, not loud** | Plain, assured statements. Show competence instead of hype. |
| **Reliable** | Precise, no vague claims. Say what is known and what is not. This fits our "Trust" theme. |
| **Human and clear** | Turn complexity into clarity. Avoid jargon and explain why. |
| **Local and context-aware** | Acknowledge country, customer and situation. |
| **Quietly essential** | The "backbone" does the work without showing off. |

Do / Don't:
- Do: "Here's the answer, here's why you can rely on it, here's who to ask."
- Do: short sentences, active verbs, concrete nouns (payslip, country, owner, last updated).
- Don't: black-box AI language ("magic", "revolutionary"), buzzwords, or over-promising.

Pitch lines that fit both the brand and the challenge:
- *Find it. Understand it. Trust it.* (challenge tagline)
- *Makes knowledge work.* (riff on the brand idea)

## 3. Visual identity

The release confirms only the following (no hex codes or font names were published):
- a **new colour palette**
- a **flexible logo system**
- **updated typography**
- a **fluid, connected, modern** design system, consistent across digital touchpoints

**Colours and fonts:** see [tokens.md](tokens.md). They come from the website's design system CSS, not the press release. The main brand blue is `#006DD8`, with magenta `#B72280` as the secondary colour, and the fonts are SD Worx Display (headings) and Inter (body).

**Website look and feel:** see [website-style.md](website-style.md) for layout, type scale, shapes, shadows, components, imagery and copy patterns.

## 4. Logo

Put the official files in [logo/](logo/). Download them from the SD Worx press or brand pages or the site header (SVG preferred). Usage rules:
- Use the logo unaltered, with clear space around it.
- Because the system is flexible, pick the variant that contrasts with the background (light or dark).
- Put it top-left in the app header and on the title and closing slides of the pitch.
- Don't recolour, stretch or add effects.

## 5. Applying it to our PoC

- Tone: calm, credible. Trust signals (recency, owner, scope, conflicts, source type, gaps, expert) should read as quiet facts.
- Layout: fluid, connected. Use cards and links between sources and people to show the "connected" idea.
- Copy: use the voice table above for every label, empty state and error.
