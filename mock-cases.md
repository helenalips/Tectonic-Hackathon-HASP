# Mock Cases: Real-Life SD Worx Client Cases

Five real-life cases from SD Worx, for use as reference/mock cases. All five come from SD Worx's own marketing pages, so expect qualitative results and few hard numbers.

## 1. CityD-WES group: pay framework after a merger
*Dated 13 April 2026*

- **Situation:** CityD-WES was formed in 2020 by merging two consultancies, which left pay discrepancies between consultants.
- **Approach:** They built a competence matrix defining functions and required criteria, then a salary scale on top of it. They also moved compensation data into a single system with automated calculations and real-time insights, replacing manual Excel forecasting.
- **Outcome:** The HR lead can make market-based offers when recruiting, run internal checks and alerts, and report on past career evolutions or forecast salary costs. The page gives no hard KPIs, only qualitative results.

## 2. Kaneka Belgium: preparing for the EU Pay Transparency Directive
*2026*

- **Situation:** Kaneka is a chemistry technology company with about 350 employees. It already had job descriptions, a job matrix, external benchmarking, the Hay evaluation method, and a previous pay equity audit with SD Worx.
- **Approach:** The directive was a wake-up call, so they set up an HR-led project team to map gaps without waiting for full legal clarity.
- **Challenges:**
  - Several data sources had to work as one system.
  - Career paths and competency frameworks needed improvement.
  - Calculating adjusted and unadjusted pay gaps proved harder than expected.
  - Managers were not yet aware of the directive, so HR is building e-learning modules, intranet resources and live sessions.
- **Outcome:** Work in progress, not a finished result. The priorities now are data quality, consistent reporting, job architecture and manager readiness.

## 3. Afriflora: HR and payroll for 15,000 employees in Ethiopia
*Likely 2025*

- **Situation:** Afriflora, the world's leading rose producer, ran payroll through huge Excel files managed from the Netherlands, with about 50 new employees joining daily. The system often crashed and was exposed to fraud and errors.
- **Approach:** They moved to one integrated HR and payroll system on SAP SuccessFactors. They chose SD Worx partly for its payroll experience in Nigeria, and SD Worx provided an Ethiopian consultant for local support.
- **Challenges:** The hard part was cultural rather than technical: a strongly hierarchical workforce unused to critically evaluating processes.
- **Outcome:** All 15,000 employees have transitioned. Data control improved, fraud risk dropped, payroll delays ended, and integrations cover biometric time tracking and bonus management. Self-service isn't live yet because many workers lack smartphones.
- **Date caveat:** The page has no explicit date. The go-live is described as "as of January", and the page assets were uploaded in 2025, so it is probably recent.

## 4. Global Paint company: 47 payroll providers down to 1
*Page assets from July 2025*

- **Situation:** A global company with 50,000 employees in 45 countries had no standardised payroll process, used 47 separate providers, and had low visibility, poor documentation and a high error rate. Employee engagement was also very low.
- **Approach:** It wanted one multinational payroll provider and set up regional shared service centres to work with SD Worx.
- **Outcome:** SD Worx coordinates managed payroll across all countries, plus partner solutions for time capture and, in some countries, HR functions. Online payslips were the first step toward employee self-service. No quantified results.
- **Date caveat:** The content isn't dated and the client is anonymised.

## 5. SK hi-tech battery materials Poland: HR system for a growing plant
*Borderline on dates*

- **Situation:** The company runs a new lithium-ion battery separator plant with nearly 500 employees and a target of 2,000.
- **Approach:** It implemented SD Worx InnovaHR, built on SAP SuccessFactors with SD Worx's own tools for reporting, document generation, time management and Polish declarations. It covered org structure, HR and payroll, time management, and performance and goals, with a data migration from the previous provider.
- **Outcome:** Bulk payslip and tax-document distribution, self-service for managers and employees, and automated Polish social security corrections. Manual effort for payslips and tax forms was practically eliminated.
- **Date caveat:** The page is dated 4 August 2026, but the implementation ran from June 2021 to February 2022. It was published recently, but the work itself is not from the last two years. Swap it out if the dates matter.

## Reliability and patterns

- Only CityD-WES is clearly dated inside the window. Kaneka is solidly recent by context. The other three need the caveats above.
- All five come from SD Worx's own marketing pages. Expect qualitative results and few hard numbers.
- Two themes run through them:
  - EU pay transparency compliance (cases 1 and 2)
  - Consolidating fragmented HR or payroll into one system (cases 3 to 5)
